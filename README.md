# Universal Shortcuts

Computers are supposed to make life easier, not harder. This repository is a simple list of custom Windows shortcuts that I keep on a USB drive. 

Instead of searching through menus, digging for install folders, or wasting time setting up every single program manually on a new PC, you just copy these shortcuts over and move on with your day. 

I built this account to keep things dead simple, cut out the unnecessary tech jargon, and share straightforward solutions that just work.

### How to Use This

1. Create your shortcuts on your computer by right-clicking on your desktop, selecting **New > Shortcut**, and pasting your program location.
2. Drag and drop those shortcut icons onto a USB drive.
3. Plug the USB into any new PC, copy the shortcuts over, and you are good to go.
